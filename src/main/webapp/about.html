KareTum Diagram

AI-Powered Diagram Creation Made Simple

What We Do

KareTum Diagram is an intelligent diagram creation tool that transforms your ideas into professional diagrams using the power of artificial intelligence. Simply describe what you need in natural language, and our AI generates beautiful, structured diagrams instantly.

AI-Powered

Describe your diagram in plain English. Our AI understands your intent and creates the perfect visual representation.

Multiple Diagram Types

Create flowcharts, sequence diagrams, UML diagrams, ER diagrams, network diagrams, and more.

Draw.io Compatible

All diagrams are generated in standard draw.io format. Export, edit, and share with full compatibility.

Privacy First

Your diagrams are created locally. We don't store your data on our servers. Optional local AI processing with Ollama.

Our Mission

We believe diagramming should be accessible to everyone. Whether you're a developer, designer, project manager, or student, KareTum Diagram removes the barrier between your ideas and professional visual documentation.

Technology

KareTum Diagram is built on the open-source draw.io editor and enhanced with AI capabilities through Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT, Anthropic Claude, and local Ollama models. This combination provides both powerful AI generation and full offline capability.
