Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

KareTum Diagram ("we", "us", or "our") collects information you provide directly:

  • Diagram Data: Diagrams you create are processed locally in your browser and are not stored on our servers.
  • AI Prompts: When you use our AI features, your prompts are sent to third-party AI providers (Google Gemini, Ollama) for processing. We do not store your prompts or AI responses.
  • Configuration: Your settings are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve the diagram creation service
  • To process your AI requests through the provider you select
  • To remember your preferences in your browser's local storage

3. Third-Party Services

Depending on the AI provider you choose, requests may be sent to:

  • Google Gemini API: Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI API: Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic API: Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Ollama (optional): Local AI processing on your own device. No data leaves your machine.
  • Custom endpoint (optional): A provider you configure; subject to its own policy.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage only to remember your settings (selected provider, model, and API key entered in the app). You can clear this at any time via your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures. Your diagrams and prompts are processed locally or through encrypted API connections. We do not store personal data on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
